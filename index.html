Acromegaly: the most reliable confirmatory test is —
A ↑ GH levels
B ↑ IGF-1 levels
C No GH suppression after glucose
D No IGF-1 suppression after glucose
You chose B · Correct is C — you've missed this twice before.
ExplanationMedium🎧 Listen
Correct answer: C — GH not suppressed after oral glucose.
Confirming acromegaly
  • The oral glucose suppression test is the confirmatory gold standard — normally glucose suppresses GH; in acromegaly it fails to suppress.
  • IGF-1 is the best screening test (reflects 24-h GH), but not confirmatory on its own.
  • Random GH is unreliable (pulsatile secretion).
