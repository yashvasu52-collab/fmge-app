@ ENT ~ Oral cavity, salivary gland & pharyngeal disease ~ ENT_ORL ~ Dhingra Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat 8e, Ch. Diseases of Oral Cavity and Pharynx
H ~ An elderly woman has dysphagia, regurgitation of undigested food, halitosis and a gurgling swelling in the left neck. Which condition is this and where does it arise? ~ A pharyngeal pouch herniating through Killian dehiscence between the two parts of the inferior constrictor ~ An oesophageal diverticulum at the carina ~ A branchial cyst of the second arch ~ A laryngocele through the thyrohyoid membrane ~ A ~ Cricopharyngeal dysfunction raises pressure above the sphincter, blind nasogastric or endoscopic instrumentation risks perforating the pouch, and treatment is endoscopic stapled diverticulotomy or open excision with myotomy.

M ~ A child born with profound bilateral sensorineural deafness has received no benefit from hearing aids at 18 months. Which intervention offers the best speech outcome and why is timing critical? ~ A bone-anchored hearing aid, timing being unimportant ~ Cochlear implantation, because auditory cortical plasticity declines sharply after the first few years ~ Repeat hearing aid trial until school age ~ Middle ear implant after the age of 6 ~ B ~ The implant bypasses non-functioning hair cells to stimulate the cochlear nerve directly, and implantation within the sensitive period gives far better spoken language than late surgery, which is the rationale for newborn screening.

What has happened? ~ Atrophic rhinitis has developed ~ Allergic rhinitis has progressed ~ Rhinitis medicamentosa from rebound vasodilatation after prolonged topical decongestant use ~ A nasal polyp has formed ~ C ~ Tachyphylaxis and rebound congestion follow use beyond about 5 to 7 days, and management is withdrawal of the decongestant under cover of an intranasal corticosteroid with saline irrigation.
